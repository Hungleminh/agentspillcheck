# ADR-0006: Minimal agent-proposed brand

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Question: Q9 (docs/PLAN.md section 3)

## Context

No logo or brand colours exist yet. README, the HTML report and the
dashboard need a consistent, minimal identity (PLAN 20.1, 13.2).

## Decision

The agent proposes a minimal identity, to be approved or replaced by
the owner later: a text wordmark "AgentSpillCheck" (no third-party
logo or artwork) and one primary colour, deep teal `#0E7C6B`, on a
neutral light/dark neutral palette. Severity colours follow the
conventional green/yellow/orange/red ramp.

Confirmed by the project owner on 2026-10-30.

## Consequences

The colour and wordmark live in one place per surface (report
templates, dashboard CSS) so a future rebrand is a small change.
No trademarked asset of another vendor may be introduced.
